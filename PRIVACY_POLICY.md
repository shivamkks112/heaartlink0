# Privacy Policy - HeartLink Dating App

**Last Updated: October 2025**

## Introduction

HeartLink ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our dating application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Profile Data:** Name, age, gender, location, bio, interests
- **Photos:** Profile pictures and additional photos
- **Contact Information:** Email address, phone number
- **Dating Preferences:** Age range, gender preferences, location preferences

### Usage Information  
- **App Activity:** Profiles viewed, likes/dislikes, matches, messages
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, app version
- **Location Data:** Approximate location for matching (with your consent)

### Communication Data
- **Messages:** Chat conversations with matched users
- **Reports:** Safety reports and blocked user information

## How We Use Your Information

### Core Features
- **Matchmaking:** AI-powered compatibility scoring and profile suggestions
- **Communication:** Enable chat with matched users
- **Safety:** Profile verification and community moderation
- **Personalization:** Customize your experience based on preferences

### Service Improvement
- **Analytics:** App performance and user experience optimization
- **Customer Support:** Respond to inquiries and technical issues
- **Safety & Security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations

## Information Sharing

### We DO NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

### Limited Sharing Scenarios:
- **Other Users:** Profile information visible to potential matches
- **Service Providers:** Trusted partners for app functionality (hosting, analytics)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect user safety
- **Business Transfers:** In case of merger or acquisition (with user notification)

## Your Privacy Controls

### Profile Visibility
- **Show Profile:** Control who can see your profile
- **Show Photos:** Manage photo visibility
- **Incognito Mode:** Browse without appearing in discovery

### Data Management
- **Account Settings:** Update or delete profile information
- **Privacy Settings:** Control information sharing preferences
- **Data Export:** Request a copy of your data
- **Account Deletion:** Delete your account and associated data

## Data Security

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Secure Servers:** Industry-standard security measures
- **Access Controls:** Limited employee access to personal data
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and updates

### User Safety Features
- **Profile Verification:** AI and manual verification processes
- **Block & Report:** Tools to manage unwanted interactions
- **Admin Moderation:** Human review of reported content

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Data may be retained for up to 2 years
- **Deleted Accounts:** Most data deleted within 30 days
- **Legal Requirements:** Some data retained longer if legally required

## Children's Privacy

HeartLink is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## International Users

- Primary servers located in India
- May transfer data internationally for service provision
- Adequate safeguards in place for international transfers

### Regional Compliance
- **India:** Compliant with Personal Data Protection Bill
- **Global:** GDPR-ready practices for international users

## Third-Party Services
