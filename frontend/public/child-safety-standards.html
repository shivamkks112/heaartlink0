Child Safety Standards

HeartLink Dating App - CSAM Prevention & Safety Policy

⚠️ Adult-Only Platform

HeartLink is strictly for users 18 years of age and older. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and take extensive measures to prevent minors from accessing our platform.

1. Age Verification & Restrictions

Minimum Age Requirement

  • 18+ Only: HeartLink requires all users to be at least 18 years old
  • Account Creation: Age verification during signup process
  • Content Rating: App rated Mature 17+ on Google Play Store
  • Immediate Suspension: Any underage accounts are immediately suspended

Age Verification Measures

  1. Date of birth verification during account creation
  2. Email/phone verification to prevent fake accounts
  3. Profile verification system with manual review
  4. Suspicious account monitoring and investigation
  5. User reporting system for suspected underage accounts

2. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Prevention

Zero Tolerance Policy

HeartLink has a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We employ multiple layers of prevention, detection, and reporting mechanisms.

Prevention Measures

  • Content Moderation: All uploaded photos undergo automated and manual review
  • Profile Verification: Multi-step verification to confirm user identity and age
  • Automated Detection: AI-powered systems to detect inappropriate content
  • User Reporting: Easy-to-use reporting system for flagging concerns
  • Immediate Removal: Instant takedown of any flagged content

Detection Systems

  • Image analysis for inappropriate content
  • Behavioral pattern monitoring
  • Keyword filtering in messages and profiles
  • Age estimation through profile analysis
  • Community reporting and flagging

3. Reporting & Response Protocol

User Reporting

Users can report concerns through multiple channels:

  • In-App Reporting: Report button on every profile and message
  • Email: help@heartlink.co.in
  • 24/7 Response: Dedicated team for child safety concerns

Response Actions

  1. Immediate Review: All CSAM reports prioritized for instant review
  2. Content Removal: Immediate takedown of flagged content
  3. Account Suspension: Permanent ban of offending accounts
  4. Law Enforcement: Mandatory reporting to authorities
  5. Evidence Preservation: All evidence preserved for legal proceedings

🚨 Mandatory Reporting

HeartLink is committed to cooperating fully with law enforcement. We report all suspected CSAM to:

  • NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children)
  • Local Law Enforcement Authorities
  • Cybercrime Departments

4. Technical Safeguards

Platform Security

  • Encrypted Communications: All data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure Storage: Protected servers with restricted access
  • Access Controls: Limited staff access to user data
  • Audit Logs: Complete tracking of all system access

Content Monitoring

  • Automated image scanning for inappropriate content
  • Pattern recognition for suspicious behavior
  • Machine learning models for age detection
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. User Education & Awareness

Community Guidelines

  • Clear terms of service emphasizing 18+ requirement
  • Prominent display of reporting mechanisms
  • Educational resources about online safety
  • Regular safety reminders to users

Safety Features

  • Block and report functionality
  • Privacy controls (incognito mode, photo privacy)
  • Verification badges for authentic profiles
  • Safety tips and guidelines

6. Compliance & Legal Framework

Legal Compliance

HeartLink complies with all applicable laws including:

  • 18 U.S.C. § 2258A: CSAM reporting requirements
  • IT Act 2000 (India): Cyber laws and regulations
  • POCSO Act (India): Protection of Children from Sexual Offences
  • COPPA: Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
  • GDPR: Data protection and privacy regulations

Regional Authorities Cooperation

We work closely with:

  • National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), India
  • Cyber Crime Investigation Cell
  • Women & Child Development Ministry
  • International law enforcement agencies

  • 24/7 Monitoring: Round-the-clock content moderation
  • Regular Training: Ongoing education on safety protocols
  • Mental Health Support: Resources for staff reviewing sensitive content
