Delete Account & Data

Request permanent deletion of your HeartLink account and associated data

⚠️ Warning: Permanent Action

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. All your data including profile, matches, messages, and photos will be permanently deleted from our servers.

What Will Be Deleted:

Profile Information

  • Name and bio
  • Profile photos
  • Interests and preferences
  • Location data

Activity Data

  • Match history
  • Chat messages
  • Likes and interactions
  • Account settings

How to Delete Your Account:

1️⃣ Delete from App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open HeartLink app
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Scroll to bottom → Click "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion → Account deleted immediately

2️⃣ Request via Email

Send us an email with your account details:

📧 Send Deletion Request Email

Email: help@heartlink.co.in
Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
Include: Your registered email address

Deletion Timeline:

  • In-App Deletion: Immediate (within seconds)
  • Email Request: Processed within 24-48 hours
  • Complete Data Removal: All data permanently deleted from servers within 30 days

Important Notes:

  • • Account deletion cannot be undone
  • • You will need to create a new account if you want to use HeartLink again
  • • Your matches will no longer see your profile
  • • All conversations will be permanently deleted
  • • Legal retention: Some data may be retained for legal compliance (30 days maximum)
