🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: December 7, 2024

At HeartLink, we respect your right to privacy and data control. This page explains how to request the deletion of your account and personal data from our platform.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your HeartLink account using any of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the HeartLink app and log in to your account
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision by entering your password
  5. Your account will be permanently deleted within 24 hours

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to help@heartlink.co.in with:

  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Your registered email address or phone number
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your request within 48 hours.

⚠️ Important Notice: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including profile information, matches, and messages, will be permanently deleted.

What Data Gets Deleted?

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • ✅ Your profile information (name, age, bio, photos)
  • ✅ All your messages and conversations
  • ✅ Your match history and likes
  • ✅ Your preferences and settings
  • ✅ Your location data and activity logs
  • ✅ Your payment history (if applicable)
  • ✅ Any verification documents submitted

Data Retention Period

After deletion request:

  • Immediate: Your profile becomes invisible to other users
  • 24 hours: Account deactivated and data deletion begins
  • 30 days: All personal data permanently deleted from our servers
  • 90 days: Backup data purged from all systems
📝 Legal Retention: Some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes (e.g., financial records for tax purposes, fraud prevention logs) as required by law. This data is securely stored and not accessible for operational use.

What Happens to My Data Shared with Others?

  • Messages you sent to other users will be deleted from your account but may remain visible to recipients
  • Other users' screenshots or saved content are outside our control
  • Public content you created (reviews, ratings) may be anonymized but not deleted

Grace Period

We offer a 14-day grace period after deletion request during which you can:

  • Cancel the deletion request
  • Recover your account by logging in
  • Download your data before permanent deletion

After 14 days, deletion becomes irreversible.

Download Your Data Before Deletion

Before deleting your account, you can download a copy of your data:

  1. Go to Settings → Privacy & Safety
  2. Tap "Download My Data"
  3. We'll email you a secure download link within 24 hours
  4. The link expires after 7 days

Your Rights Under Data Protection Laws

Under GDPR, CCPA, and Indian IT Act, you have the right to:

  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Access: Obtain a copy of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Portability: Transfer data to another service
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing
